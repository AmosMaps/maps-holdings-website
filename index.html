Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you WhatsApp us.

Is the R500 room deposit refundable?

Yes, provided:

  • You give notice at least 7 days before moving out (on or before the 25th)
  • The room is left clean
  • The mattress is left clean
  • The property is not damaged — if damage exceeds the deposit amount, the balance must be settled
  • All keys are returned
  • No cockroach infestation is left behind
  • No lease clause has been violated

Refunds are paid on or before the 4th of the following month.

How do I know if a room is still available?

Availability is shown live on this page — check the green "Available" or red "Fully Booked" badge on each room type in the Rooms & Pricing section, or WhatsApp us to confirm.

Do all rooms have a private bathroom?

Standard and Missing Middle rooms share showers & toilets. A Drop In The Ocean has a private shower, and Bachelor Rooms have a private shower and toilet.

What happens during load shedding or a water outage?

Every room uses prepaid electricity so you manage your own usage, and we keep backup JoJo water tanks on site so water supply isn't interrupted.

Can I drop off my laundry myself, or do you collect?

Both — drop off at Maps Laundromat directly, or book our collect & deliver service for a small fee via the booking form or WhatsApp.

How do I actually book a room or laundry slot?

Message us on WhatsApp, or use the enquiry / booking buttons on this site — we'll confirm all the details with you directly since everything runs by appointment.
